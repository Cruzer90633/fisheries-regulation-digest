# Project Context

This project is the **Fisheries Regulation Digest** — a public-facing app that tracks fishery regulation updates from the Mid-Atlantic Fishery Management Council (MAFMC) and NOAA Fisheries Greater Atlantic Regional Fisheries Office (GARFO), and turns them into short, plain-English summaries.

The goal: make it easy for students, industry members, and the public to know when a rule affects a species or region they care about, without having to dig through dense regulatory PDFs and mailing lists.

V1 scope:
- Pull announcements from MAFMC and GARFO
- Summarize each one in plain English (what changed, species/area affected, effective date)
- Let users filter by species or region
- Link every summary back to its original source
- Keep an archive/search of past digests

Out of scope for V1: legal interpretation, compliance advice, real-time enforcement data.
